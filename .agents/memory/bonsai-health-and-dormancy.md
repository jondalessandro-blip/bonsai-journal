---
name: Bonsai health and dormancy
description: The product distinction between seasonal dormancy and health status.
---

Track seasonal dormancy independently from health status. Entering or leaving dormancy must never overwrite or reinterpret the tree's health status.

**Why:** A tree can be dormant while healthy, stressed, or otherwise in a health state; dormancy and health are distinct facts.

**How to apply:** Keep separate fields, filters, badges, and history behavior whenever changing Bonsai Journal's tree records.
