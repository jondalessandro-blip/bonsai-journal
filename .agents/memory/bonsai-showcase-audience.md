---
name: Bonsai showcase audience
description: Product guidance for public Bonsai Journal showcase content.
---

Showcase content should present a range that includes young, ordinary, and in-progress trees, not only highly refined specimens.

**Why:** The creator wants novices with young trees in development to feel welcome rather than intimidated by a showcase of only polished bonsai.

**How to apply:** When curating public showcase images or copy, value realistic progress and beginner accessibility; avoid implying that only finished trees are worth documenting.
